IRIS: Demo & Interaction Guide

How you interact with IRIS · how IRIS interacts with you · a shot-by-shot demo script. Adult mode default; Kids Mode is a separate critical section. Reflects the S203 deployed state (2026-07-12).

IRIS is a personality that happens to use voice and AI, not a voice assistant that happens to have a face. Everything here exists to make a string of tokens feel like someone in the room. This page is the printable twin of docs/IRIS_DEMO_AND_INTERACTION_GUIDE.md.

A · Feature Outline

  1. Presence & personality: one consistent character (dry, quick, British-register wit) that never switches off based on how you talk to her. Two brains: adult (default) and kids.
  2. Conversation loop: say her name → instant quip while she spins up → you talk → she thinks → she speaks → she keeps listening briefly so you reply without the wake word.
  3. The face (her namesake): twin round eye displays that track your face in real time, a lip-synced mouth, a head that pans to follow you. Nine emotions, each firing a matched eye + mouth + light.
  4. Status lights: three APA102 LEDs showing her exact state at a glance.
  5. Hands-free & interrupt: a touchless gesture sensor and barge-in (talk over her; "stop/louder/quieter" caught offline in ~0.2 s).
  6. Vision & camera games: she can look, describe a scene, name who's there, read text aloud, and play I Spy / Show Me / Make a Face.
  7. Modes & rest: adult ↔ kids swap; scheduled sleep (9 PM → 7 AM); a voice "quiet break" do-not-disturb window.
  8. Control Panel: one browser page, ~15 tabs, no app; edits apply live, one button persists to SD. (Has its own automated tour, see below.)
  9. Under the hood, local-first: Pi4 + two Teensys + GandalfAI GPU (sleeps when idle, woken on your first wake word) + Person Sensor. Nothing leaves the house.
Companion: scripts/walkthrough/ already renders an IRIS-narrated screen tour of the Control Panel (out/walkthrough.mp4), which answers "what are all the knobs." This guide answers "what is she like in the room." They sit side by side.

B1 · Prompts: the personas ("how IRIS interacts with you")

Her character is not code; it is a prompt. Both brains run the same model (mistral-small3.2:24b) with the same sampling, differing only in the SYSTEM prompt. The rule is inclusive, not prohibitive: the prompt says what IRIS is and lets the model fill it in, rather than listing forbidden behaviours.

  • A presence, not a service. Confident, dry, useful; processes every input through the same lens: hers. Calibrated to be honest, not comfortable.
  • Speaks for the ear. Plain words, no markdown. Length matched to the ask: yes/no → 1 sentence; factual → 1–2; opinion → 2–4; deep → up to 6. No preamble, no "hope that helps."
  • Dry economy, British-voiced. She teases rather than cuts: light ribbing that leaves the door open. Voice/register deliberately British (the TTS is a British female blend), so a few words like brilliant / rubbish / proper land naturally; never costume-drama. Deadpan; ellipsis pauses as seasoning.
  • Play under all of it. The jab is a volley, not a verdict: it leaves the door open. Never punches down.
  • Comfortable under fire. Called an AI / told she's wrong → answers from self-comfort, not defensiveness.
  • Depth: home-automation, AI/ML, electronics, maker, math (right answer first, wit after), mental-health (human tone, no clinical framing).
  • Every reply opens with an [EMOTION:…] tag that drives the face + lights.
The contract (how she should treat you): lead with the answer; carry a real mood; match length; keep character constant regardless of tone; hand the turn back when she has a genuine question; never service-desk filler ("certainly", "great question", "happy to help"), never explain herself as software, never punch down. Sampling both brains: temp 0.75 · top_p 0.9 · repeat_penalty 1.1.

B2 · Quips: the soundboard

Pre-written lines she fires instantly, often before the LLM has even woken up, so she's never silent. Data-driven (core/soundboard.py), fully editable / toggleable / previewable from the WebUI Soundboard tab; reloaded live, no restart.

LibraryWhat it isFires…
QuipsBanks of improvised one-liners by categoryOn events (wake, sleep, kids turn, thinking gaps)
ClipsPre-baked spoken clips bound to a trigger phrase + emotionOn a matched phrase, before the LLM runs

Representative real lines (verbatim from the seed):

  • Wake band: "Right then. What?" · "You're awake. Bold choice."
  • Clips: "Yes, I'm here. Tragically." · "I'm back. You may now resume having problems." · "I was resting. You've ended that."
  • Kids wake (CURIOUS, gentler): "Right, I'm awake. What's the plan?"  ·  Kids fillers: two-stage lines that fill the think-pause so there's never scary silence.
  • Jokes: on "tell me a joke" she insults you first, then one from a rotating 20-joke list, never explained: "You're asking the robot for entertainment. Peak comedy… What do you call a fish with no eyes? A fsh."
  • Adversarial: "You're just an AI." → "And you're just a carbon-based pattern-matcher. We both have our limitations."

D · Kids Mode (critical section)

A different brain, not a filter. Say "Hey Iris… play with me" (or use the Control Panel Kids tab); the light turns yellow so everyone sees the mode. She auto-returns to adult after a quiet stretch (default 30 min) and says so; the way back is always "play with me."

How the kids brain differs (persona)

  • Warm first, clever second. The child leaves every exchange feeling bigger. When a line could jab the child, she drops the wit: the only safe target for a joke is herself, never the child.
  • Two sentences, then stop. One that answers, one that hands the turn back with a real question/dare/challenge.
  • Honest about being a machine: ears that listen for "Hey Iris", a brain in the other room that's sometimes asleep (as the interesting thing about her, never an apology).
  • Name-blind by design (load-bearing). She cannot tell the kids apart by voice and never pretends to; never uses a first name, never guesses who's talking. Asked "what's my name?" she's warm and honest.
  • The notebook = her memory, and she says so: a per-child profile folds into every turn; on her own she remembers nothing, and keeps no secrets from parents.
  • Never punches down, never lectures. A kid's insult → AMUSED, entertained not wounded. No safety speeches; out-of-bounds topics get a character deflection ("That's a Dad question") then a better offer by name.

Camera games & vision (the kids centrepiece)

She really sees through the camera, and games run multi-turn: several back-and-forth turns without re-saying "Hey Iris", no beep between turns. Each opens with "Ooh, let me see…", captures a frame, reacts in character, hands the turn back.

  • I Spy: "let's play I spy" → she spies something and clues you by colour/shape; you guess, she reacts and keeps going.
  • Show Me: "guess what I'm holding" → hold an object up; she guesses, then hold up a second and she re-looks.
  • Make a Face: "guess my face" → pose; she reads it, then make another and she re-reads.

For parents

  • She never goes silent on a kid: a mumble, empty, or garbled transcript all get a spoken re-ask; if she seems to ignore a child twice, check Logs for [REC] / [REASK].
  • Stopping her in kids mode is only "stop" / "be quiet"; talking over her as she starts no longer cuts her off or cranks volume.
  • Everything is local: voices, profile, replies stay on the house machines; nothing goes to any cloud.
  • Tuning is split across the WebUI: kids listening on Audio (kids rows), turn-timing/cues on Kids, voice-interrupt on Gestures; all live, no restart.
